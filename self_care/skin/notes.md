# Face Acne

Your Evening Step-by-Step Routine
When introducing the retinoid, your night routine should look like this:
1. Cleanse: Wash your forehead and face with a basic, gentle face wash and warm water.
2. Dry Completely: Pat your skin dry with a clean towel and wait 2 to 3 minutes. Applying retinoids to damp skin increases how quickly it absorbs, which can trigger severe burning or peeling.
3. The Buffer (Recommended): Apply a thin layer of your Aveeno Face Lotion first. Let it dry for a few minutes. This creates a protective shield to keep your skin barrier from throwing a tantrum.
4. Apply Retinoid: Smooth a pea-sized amount of the 0.1% adapalene gel across your entire forehead.
5. Lock It In: Apply one more light layer of your Aveeno moisturizer to lock everything in for the night.
